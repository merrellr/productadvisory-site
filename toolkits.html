What this is

Practical tools built from real product work

These templates are the same ones we use with clients. Each one is a fillable PDF with a matching Google Sheet/Excel, so you can work on screen, in a spreadsheet, or on your phone. Every tool includes a worked example and clear instructions, so you are never starting from a blank page.

Why it matters

Better decisions, easier to defend

Most product decisions are made fast, with opinions in the room and little written down. That works until someone asks why. A simple structure gets the real criteria on the table, shows where your team disagrees, and leaves a record you can stand behind later. These tools give you that structure in about an hour, not a week.

How to use it

Three steps to a decision you can defend

  1. Pick a tool that fits your level or the problem in front of you.
  2. Look at the example first. Spend five minutes with it before you fill in your own.
  3. Fill it in with your team. Have two people score on their own, then compare. The gaps are where the real conversation is.
