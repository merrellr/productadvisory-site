Free e-books

Read on your Kindle, Kobo or iPad

Every Product+ article and The Product Manager's Playbook come as e-books (EPUB files). The text fits your screen, and your reader's own fonts, sizes and night mode all work.

iPad, iPhone or Mac

  1. Tap Download on the e-book you want.
  2. Open the file and choose Books (on iPhone and iPad, from the Share button).
  3. It's in your library, on every Apple device you use.

Kindle

  1. Download the e-book.
  2. Send it with Amazon's Send to Kindle: drag it onto amazon.com/sendtokindle↗ (opens in a new tab), share it to the Kindle app on your phone, or email it to your Kindle's address.
  3. Amazon converts it, and it appears on your Kindle and in the Kindle app.

Emailing it? Add your own address to the approved list in your Amazon account first (Manage your content and devices, then Preferences, then Personal document settings).

Kobo

  1. Download the e-book on your computer.
  2. Connect your Kobo by USB and drag the file onto it, or save it to your Dropbox or Google Drive if you've linked one to your Kobo.
  3. Eject the Kobo; the book is in your library.

Android and others

  1. Download the e-book.
  2. Open it in Google Play Books (choose Upload files) or any e-book app.

Prefer a fixed page you can print? Every book is also a PDF.
