Step by step

Choose your device.

iPhone or iPad (Apple Podcasts)
  1. Tap Apple Podcasts above. The Podcasts app opens on the show.
  2. Tap Follow (the + button). New episodes arrive by themselves.
  3. If the app doesn't open: in the Podcasts app, go to Library, tap the ⋯ button at the top, choose Follow a Show by URL, and paste the feed address.
Mac (Apple Podcasts)
  1. Click Apple Podcasts above, or open the Podcasts app.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File, then Follow a Show by URL.
  3. Paste the feed address and click Follow.
Spotify
  1. Tap Spotify above, or search Spotify for Product+ Audio, then tap Follow.
Android
  1. Tap Pocket Casts above if you use it.
  2. In most Android podcast apps (Podcast Addict, AntennaPod, Podverse): tap + or Add, choose RSS feed or Add by URL, and paste the feed address.
Any other app
  1. Tap Any other app above to copy the feed address.
  2. In your app, look for Add by URL, Add RSS feed or Follow a show by URL, and paste it.
