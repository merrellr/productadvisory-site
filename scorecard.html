Feature Prioritization Scorecard

Rank features with criteria you can change, or with RICE, and compare the two, so your roadmap reflects your goals and not the loudest voice. About 20 minutes, no sign-up, and nothing leaves your browser.

1

Choose what matters

Start from a template, then rename, reweight, add or remove criteria. Weight 0 switches one off.

2

Score your features

1 is low and 5 is high. For criteria marked lower is better, like effort or risk, 5 means a lot of it. Tip: have two people score on their own, then compare.

1

How RICE works

A fixed formula from Intercom's product team, best for ranking many similar items in one product area.

Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort

  • Reach: how many people it affects in a set period, for example users per quarter. Use the same period for every feature.
  • Impact: how much it moves each of them: massive (3), high (2), medium (1), low (0.5) or minimal (0.25).
  • Confidence: how sure you are of the other numbers: high (100%), medium (80%) or low (50%).
  • Effort: how much work it takes, in person-months.

    Make it stick

    • Argue about weights, not scores. Agree what matters first; the ranking follows.
    • Write down why. The CSV or PDF is your record when someone asks later.
    • Re-run it each quarter. Goals change, and so should the weights.
