About Us

Who we are, and how we think about product.

Product Advisory exists because too many good teams build the wrong things well. Not from a lack of talent or effort, but because strategy, evidence, and execution too often live in separate conversations that never quite connect. That gap is what I help close.

Product Advisory is a product management consulting firm built to help startups and enterprises turn market insight into products people actually want.

We started with a simple belief: too many products fail not because teams can't build, but because they build the wrong thing. Our approach starts outside your organization, in the market and with your end users, before a single roadmap decision gets made.

Our team brings over 50 years of combined technology and product experience to every engagement, spanning healthcare, data strategy, and enterprise software. We've built product organizations from the ground up, scaled them through growth, and know what it takes to go from an early-stage idea to an enterprise-ready platform.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all consulting. Whether you need a single strategic engagement or an embedded, end-to-end product partner, we scale our involvement to match what you need, complementing your team's strengths rather than replacing them.
