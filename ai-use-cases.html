AI Use Case Prioritization

List your AI ideas, answer five questions for each, and see which to pilot first, which to fix before you start, and which to park. About 15 minutes, no sign-up, and nothing leaves your browser.

1

Five questions for each idea

Score each from 1 to 5. Risk counts the other way: 5 means a lot of it.

Business value

How much would it move a goal that matters: time saved, revenue, a better customer experience?

1 Nice to have5 Moves a key goal
Data readiness

Is the data there, good enough, and yours to use?

1 We'd have to find or fix it5 Ready and approved
Cost & effort

How easy is it to build or buy?

1 A custom build5 Switch on a tool
Team readiness

Will people use it, and does someone own it?

1 No owner, poor fit5 Owner and demand
Risk if wrong

How bad is it when the AI gets it wrong: privacy, compliance, customers, money?

1 Easy to catch, low harm5 Real harm
2

Score your AI ideas

One idea per row, as specific as you can: "summarize support tickets for the weekly review" beats "AI for support". The map and plan below update as you go.

3

Where they land

Value against feasibility. Numbers match the plan.

    4

    Your plan

    Best first. Priority is the average of value and feasibility, less a penalty for high risk.

      Make it stick

      • Score with the people who'd use it. Team readiness is a guess until they've weighed in.
      • Pilot one thing at a time. One team, four to six weeks, one number to judge it by.
      • Re-run it after each pilot. What you learn changes the scores for the rest.
